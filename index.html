Build with AI.Ship forUChicago.

AIUC Software Lab is for students who want to learn AI-assisted development and make apps, websites, and tools in small teams.

What members do

01

Learn AI-assisted software development.

02

Form a small team around a useful problem.

03

Build a working version and test it.

04

Publish the project and keep improving it.

Our first group project

A UChicago platform where students can publish software, find testers, and meet collaborators.

It does not exist yet. Members will design and build it together.

Who can join

Developers, designers, product thinkers, testers, and beginners. You do not need an idea or prior experience.
